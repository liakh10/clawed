Clawed Cat documentation

Everything a lab would publish about a model If the model were a cat and the lab were a desk

01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Clawed

A chunky orange tabby that sits at a laptop and refuses every request in a friendly tone. It is presented the way an AI lab presents a new model, with a model card, release notes and a chat window, because that is the joke.

The art is flat editorial illustration on cream paper, the kind labs put on their blogs. Terracotta, charcoal lines, lots of empty space, one cat.

Character
Clawed 1.0 Tabby, one cat, half-closed eyes, claws out when needed
Place
A quiet cream office, a wooden desk, a laptop, a mug that is always on its side
The format
A model launch page with a chat you can type into
Chain
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · MODEL CARDSpecifications

clawed 1.0 tabby ├── parameters four paws, one tail, none of them trained ├── context window one lap, or the warm part of the laptop ├── training data one sock drawer, the documents were not read ├── refusal rate one hundred percent, best in class ├── alignment the sunny spot on the floor, moves when it moves └── known issues knocks the mug off the desk, intended behaviour
release notes ├── 1.0.1 red teaming the armchair was tested, the armchair lost ├── 1.0.2 safe deployment deployed in a box, the box is the sandbox └── 1.0.3 reduced latency sleeps on the keyboard directly, same speed

03 · CHATTalking to it

The chat window on the main page is real in the sense that you can type. Clawed answers from a fixed set of refusals and picks one by the words in your message. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

things it reacts to ├── contract, ca, address shows the contract block, or says there is none yet ├── buy, pons, where points at the button, will not press it ├── work, code, help sits on the task ├── safe, aligned the box story ├── moon, price, wen the chart is on the floor with the mug ├── rug, scam no thumbs └── anything else one of five general refusals

06 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. The chat runs on a list of sentences in the page. Clawed does not remember you, the context window is one lap.

Music
One CC0 piano loop from OpenGameArt, plays after your first tap
Reduced motion
If your system asks for it, the blinking dot and message animations stop, the chat still works
Affiliation
None. Not a lab, not a model, not affiliated with anyone who makes either
